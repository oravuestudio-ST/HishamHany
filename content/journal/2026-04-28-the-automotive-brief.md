Cars are the hardest thing I photograph, and the most honest. A body panel is a mirror,
and a mirror never lies about the light you gave it.

## The surface is the subject

With fashion you light the person. With automotive you light the room, because the metal
only shows you what surrounds it. A clean reflection is a clean environment — gradients
shaped, edges controlled, nothing in the scene that the car doesn't deserve to wear.

## Patience is the technique

The work is slow on purpose. I move one flag, study the highlight roll across a fender,
move it again. The frame that looks effortless took the longest to build. That is the
trade every serious brief asks for: time spent before the shutter, so nothing has to be
rescued after it.
